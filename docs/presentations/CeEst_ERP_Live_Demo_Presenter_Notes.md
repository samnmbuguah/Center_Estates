# CeEst ERP live demo presenter notes

This presentation treats the uploaded videos as the client’s intended presentation sequence. It treats the attached navigation brief as a product requirement, not proof that every illustrated screen is live.

## Recommended live story

1. Log in as the resident property/facilities manager and confirm the organization and selected property.
2. Open Companion and choose one actionable item.
3. Show the property structure, one unit billing schedule and its active tenancy.
4. Show how rent and recurring charges feed invoicing; open one invoice and receipt.
5. Enter the tenant portal and submit a low-risk maintenance issue.
6. Record the generated request reference, then open the same record in Facility Management.
7. Explain triage, SLA, assignment, evidence, approval and closure. Avoid completing destructive or financially material actions during the live demo.
8. Open one asset passport and its PPM/compliance history.
9. Explain Service Profile as WHAT and Service Provider as WHO; show property allocation and SLA/KPI relationship.
10. Finish with performance and a landlord-ready report, then return to Companion.

## Opening script

“We have taken over this property. CeEst creates one operating record from the property and tenant structure through billing, maintenance, compliance, performance and landlord reporting. I will show one transaction moving through that complete chain rather than give you a tour of disconnected screens.”

## Proof language

“Here is where the requirement is defined. Here is the transaction it generated. Here is the staff or provider action. Here is the evidence and verification. Here is the KPI. And here is the same information in the management report.”

## Claims to avoid unless verified immediately before the meeting

- Do not claim a particular smart-meter brand is integrated. Explain the API/integration boundary.
- Do not claim emails, SMS, WhatsApp, payment gateway callbacks or scheduled jobs are live unless those external services are configured and a test message/transaction succeeds.
- Do not use exact SLA targets unless they are configured in the selected property.
- Do not show empty dashboards; switch to a prepared property or use the backup presentation.

## Contingency sequence

If connectivity or authentication is unstable, use slides 3–12 as the story, show the supplied walkthrough video, then return to the live application only for the tenant issue workflow and one report export.

## Five-minute pre-flight

- Sign in in a clean browser window and leave the session open.
- Confirm organization, property, light/dark theme and logo.
- Open the exact tabs required and verify they have data.
- Submit one disposable test issue and confirm its reference appears in Facility Management.
- Open one PDF report and check logo, property, period, headings and table contrast.
- Keep the PowerPoint, walkthrough video and a PDF copy locally available.
