# Property Management

Manages the commercial/operational record from property and unit setup through tenants, leases, billing, collection, expenses and reporting. See [UI_REFERENCE.md](docs/UI_REFERENCE.md).

## Functional scope

- Portfolio dashboard: occupancy, properties, units, tenants, monthly collection, arrears and lease expiry.
- Property/building/unit register with type, location, ownership, manager, amenities, photos and occupancy.
- Guided property creation with unit mix and bulk unit generation.
- Tenant biodata, contacts, tax/identity details, billing preferences, risk/status and document onboarding.
- Lease register with dates, rent, deposit, escalation, billing frequency, expiry and renewal workflow.
- Rent schedules, invoices, receipts, allocations, arrears, aging, reminders and reconciliation.
- Credit/debit adjustments and immutable receipt references.
- Annual budgets, expense categories, actual-versus-budget and approval.
- Service-charge methods, allocation ratios, budgets, reconciliation and tenant statements.
- Property expenses and supplier invoices with approval and payment status.
- Report catalogue and portfolio income/expense analytics.
- Tenant portal for statements, payments, maintenance requests, documents and messages.
