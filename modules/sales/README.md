# Sales Management

Auditable property sales for completed and under-construction projects, supporting self-financed and bank-financed buyers.

- Source specification: `specification.pdf` (Sales-Module-UIs.pdf, 19 mockup screens).
- UI reference: [docs/UI_REFERENCE.md](docs/UI_REFERENCE.md).

## Roles

Developer/owner, sales agent, site sales clerk, buyer, bank/financier, advocate/legal team and relationship manager. Each role is restricted by organization, assigned project/unit and workflow action.

## Functional scope

- Multi-project/unit setup with code, floor, type, price, availability and construction milestone linkage.
- Buyer biodata, ID/KRA/contact/photo, unique reference, unit mapping and secure confirmation.
- Online payment links for invoices/installments and automatic reconciliation/receipts.
- Manual bank/paybill proof submission, duplicate detection, full/partial verification and rejection reasons.
- Agent KPIs: volume/value, conversion, time-to-close, cancellations and project comparison.
- Site visit/QR registration, interests, notes, follow-up and introducing-agent attribution.
- On-site project, availability, pricing, process and finance presentations.
- Commission reference, payment/milestone validation, approval and payout status.
- Live marketing content, sold/available visualization and project-progress media.
- Immutable legal documents tied to buyer and unit.
- Developer, agent, clerk, buyer and financier dashboards plus compliance reporting.
