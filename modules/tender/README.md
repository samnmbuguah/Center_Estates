# Tender Management

Tender intelligence and controlled bid production from invitation to award/regret. The UI describes an 11-stage gated workflow.

## Workflow

1. Invitation registration, PDF upload/import, owner and deadlines.
2. AI extraction of mandatory requirements, criteria, forms and risks with human confirmation.
3. Bid/no-bid eligibility, profitability, risk scoring and board approval.
4. Tasks, milestones, owners, Gantt planning and notifications.
5. Reusable company-document vault with versions and expiry alerts.
6. Technical proposal templates, AI-assisted drafting, attachments, comments and collaboration.
7. Restricted financial proposal workspace, BOQ imports, pricing tables and approvals.
8. Final review, executive summary, readiness score and approval chain.
9. Packaging, validation, table of contents, ordering and final archive.
10. Submission portal/channel, receipt, timestamps, version and compliance record.
11. Award/regret outcome, value, competitors, feedback, lessons and knowledge base.

Dashboard requirements include KPIs, funnel, calendar, alerts, tasks, recent tenders and AI insights. See [UI_REFERENCE.md](docs/UI_REFERENCE.md).
